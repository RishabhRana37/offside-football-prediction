Core Capabilities

Built for Football Analytics

Six distinct intelligence layers work together to deliver state-of-the-art goal scoring predictions.

🎯
Expected Goals (xG) Engine
Models historical average xG, npxG, and xGChain scaled to match participation ratio for precise in-match goal probability calculations.
🏦
Market Intelligence
Incorporates player market value, peak value ratios, and value trajectory to quantify player quality and form signals from financial data.
🎓
International Cap Efficiency
Encodes international experience and goals-per-cap efficiency, capturing elite player quality signals that go beyond club performance data.
🔀
Multi-Smoothing Target Encoding
Out-of-fold smoothed target encoding with m∈{5,20,50} for player names, clubs, stadiums, and referees — eliminates leakage while preserving signal.
⚖️
4-Model Rank Ensemble
Optimal blending of 2× CatBoost (depth 6-7) and 2× LightGBM via greedy rank-averaging weight search across 5-fold cross validation predictions.
🛡️
Domain Post-Processing
Enforces football rules — players with 0 minutes played cannot score. Applies a deterministic zero-override rule that boosts AP measurably.

Data Ingestion & Feature Pipeline
Load 1.18M appearance records. Parse temporal features (year, month, day of week). Apply categorical encoding and football-domain ratios.
2
Advanced Feature Engineering
Create 40+ new features: xG×minutes_ratio, total_threat (xG+xA), final_third (xGChain−xGBuildup), prime_age_xG, height×xG, attacker interactions.
3
Multi-Smoothing Target Encoding
OOF smoothed encoding at m=5,20,50 for players, clubs, stadiums, referees, confederations + contextual interaction TE (player×club, player×competition).
4
Ensemble Training & Blending
Train 4 diverse models on 5-fold CV. Greedy grid search over rank-average and linear blend weights selects optimal final ensemble.
5
Post-Processing & Submission
Force zero probability for 0-minutes-played appearances. Generate solution.csv with appearance_id → scored_flag probability mapping.
